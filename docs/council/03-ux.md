# Phase 1 — UX Direction (UI/UX Lead)

Binding: `00-contract.md`. This doc says how the app looks and behaves. Product decides *what* features ship; this doc decides *how they feel*.

**North star:** a Bangladeshi tuition teacher opens the app between batches, takes roll call in under 20 seconds, sees who owes money, and gets it collected. He never reads a manual. Every screen answers three questions without being asked: *Where am I? What is the one thing to do here? Did it work?*

## 0. Six design laws (apply to every screen)
1. **One primary action per screen.** It sits at the bottom, within thumb reach (sticky button or FAB). On desktop it is top-right and has a keyboard shortcut.
2. **Smart defaults, never blank forms.** Attendance starts as all present. The payment amount starts at the fee. The month starts at the oldest unpaid month. The batch's weekdays start from today's weekday.
3. **Warm colours mean money, cool colours mean attendance.** Dues use yellow → orange → red → maroon. Attendance uses green / ink-slate / cyan / grey. The two palettes never mix.
4. **Never colour alone.** Every status has an icon and a text label, and colour comes third.
5. **Everything is undoable or confirmed.** Small actions get a snackbar with *Undo* (8 s). Destructive or bulk actions get a dialog that states the consequence in numbers.
6. **Atmosphere behind, legibility in front.** The mesh glow, grain and frosted glass live in the background and chrome. Every list, row, form, table, dialog and status badge sits on a near-opaque flat surface (§4 Legibility law).

### Visual concept — **Aurora Ink** (অরোরা ইঙ্ক; internal name)
The feel is a calm, premium instrument: ink-dark (or soft porcelain) ground, a faint aurora of violet, teal and blush light pooled at the edges, a whisper of film grain, and frosted-glass cards floating above it. Numbers are big and confident.
- **Three layers, back to front:**
  1. Static **mesh glow** (3 radial pools).
  2. **Film grain** overlay.
  3. **Frosted-glass** cards, nav and sheets. Dense content inside them sits on near-opaque fills.
- **Restraint — this is not "wedding-day lighting":** pools are static and soft and fade to 0. No rainbow, no gold or metallic, no glitter, no neon, no glows around text, and no animated gradients.
  - Exactly **one** saturated surface per screen: the hero card, on Today and onboarding only.
  - Everything else gets its colour from the mesh seen through glass.
- **Theme switch:** **সিস্টেম / লাইট / ডার্ক** (System / Light / Dark). It is a 3-segment control with mini preview swatches, and switching crossfades over 200 ms.

## 1. Information architecture

### Top-level destinations (5)
| # | বাংলা | English | Icon | What lives here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | আজ | Today | `today` | Hero "next class" card; dues summary bar; **the class schedule** (phone: day strip + day timeline; desktop: দিন/সপ্তাহ toggle with a real week grid); roll call entry; quick actions; this month's collection |
| 2 | ব্যাচ | Batches | `groups` | All batches grouped under their Subject/Class → batch detail (students, attendance, fees, **schedule & reminder**) |
| 3 | শিক্ষার্থী | Students | `person` | All students (search, filter by batch/status) → student profile |
| 4 | বেতন | Fees | `payments` | Two segments: **বকেয়া** (Dues, the default) and **আদায়** (Collection: month summary and payment log). The badge shows the overdue count. |
| 5 | আরও | More | `menu` | A short "সংরক্ষণাগার" block (Archived batches, Students who left, Help), then the **Settings** groups inline (§3a): Appearance, Center info, Classes & reminders, Fees, Messages, Backup, About |

**Where the schedule lives:** there is no 6th tab. The schedule is split by how it is used:
- **Day-to-day use (Today tab):** "what's on, and what changed today". Per-date cancel, reschedule and extra classes all start from a class card.
- **The recurring rule (Batch detail › সময়সূচি tab):** the weekly slots, the reminder lead time, and the list of upcoming one-off changes.

One-off changes are made only from class cards. Permanent changes are made only from the batch. The two never mix.

**Hierarchy:** Subject/Class (বিষয়/শ্রেণি, e.g. "গণিত — দশম শ্রেণি") → Batch (ব্যাচ, e.g. "ব্যাচ ক · সকাল ৭টা") → Students.
- **Subject/Class is never its own destination.** It appears as a group header on the Batches tab and as an inline "pick or create" field in the batch form. The teacher never has to go somewhere first to create a subject. This prevents the classic "I can't add a batch because I didn't make a subject" dead end.
- A **student is one person** with one profile and one or more **enrolments** (student × batch, with an optional fee override). Payments and attendance attach to the enrolment. The profile shows every batch as a section.
- **Global search** (magnifier in the top bar on every tab; `Ctrl+F` on desktop, with `Ctrl+K` as an alias) finds students by name, roll, or phone (Bangla or English digits), plus batches.

### Adaptive layout
Windows desktop is **first-class from day one**. The full per-screen spec is in **§3b Desktop & adaptive layout**. In short:
- Compact (< 600): floating frosted bottom-nav pill, single pane.
- Medium (600–839): frosted nav rail, 2-column tiles or cards.
- Expanded (840–1199): frosted rail; list–detail panes and a 12-column bento on Today.
- Large / extra-large (≥ 1200): extended rail with labels; list–detail–context panes and data tables.
- Sheets become right-side panels or dialogs on ≥ 840. **Extra width always becomes extra panes or columns, never side margins.**

Phone rule: interactive controls sit in the bottom 60% of the screen. The top bar holds only the title, search, and the language chip (**বাং | EN**, a one-tap switch that is always visible on the Today screen).

## 2. Key flows (minimum taps)

### (a) First run — 4 short screens, about 2 minutes
All onboarding screens show the full mesh + grain backdrop, with glass cards holding the controls. Form fields inside sit on `surfaceDense`.
1. **Language.** Two huge cards: **বাংলা** (pre-selected) / **English**. A theme row underneath offers সিস্টেম / লাইট / ডার্ক (System is pre-selected). One tap → next. *(1 tap)*
2. **"আপনার প্রথম ব্যাচ তৈরি করুন"** — a single scrolling form with a live preview card at the top:
   - বিষয়/শ্রেণি: text field plus suggestion chips (গণিত, ইংরেজি, পদার্থবিজ্ঞান, রসায়ন, জীববিজ্ঞান, ICT · ষষ্ঠ…দ্বাদশ, SSC, HSC). Tapping a subject chip and then a class chip gives "গণিত — দশম শ্রেণি".
   - ব্যাচের নাম: pre-filled "ব্যাচ ১" (editable).
   - **কোন দিন, কখন?** (the slot editor, §2g):
     - Weekday chips শনি রবি সোম মঙ্গল বুধ বৃহঃ শুক্র (the Bangladeshi week starts on Saturday). Today's day is pre-selected.
     - Start time (the Bangla time picker, defaulting to the next full hour).
     - Duration chips [১ ঘণ্টা ●] [দেড় ঘণ্টা] [২ ঘণ্টা] [অন্য].
     - The toggle **"সব দিনে একই সময়"** is ON by default. Turning it off reveals per-day time rows, and each day can take **"+ আরেকটি সময়"** for a second class that day.
     - A live sentence confirms the choice: "প্রতি শনি, সোম, বুধ — সন্ধ্যা ৭:০০ থেকে ৮:০০".
   - মাসিক ফি: number field with a ৳ prefix. It accepts ০-৯ and 0-9.
   - বেতন কখন দেয়?: two big radio cards (see Trap #3). **"মাসের শুরুতে (অগ্রিম)"** is the default. **"মাস শেষে (পরে)"** is the alternative. Each card shows a worked example.
   - Sticky **[পরবর্তী]**.
3. **"শিক্ষার্থী যোগ করুন"** — a quick-add loop with only **নাম** and **অভিভাবকের ফোন** (roll is auto-numbered ০১, ০২…). [যোগ করুন] adds the row to the list below and keeps the keyboard open on the name field. Secondary: **"পরে যোগ করব"**. Sticky **[পরবর্তী]**.
4. **Reminder priming** (§2h) → **[হ্যাঁ, চালু করুন]** or **[এখন না]** → Today screen with a one-time coach-mark on the class card: "হাজিরা নিতে এখানে চাপুন".

Empty states everywhere teach the next step with one button (see §7).

### (b) Roll call in < 20 s — 4–5 taps
Today → tap **[হাজিরা নিন]** on the class card, or tap the class reminder notification *(1)* → roll call screen opens with **everyone present** → tap the 2–3 absent rows *(2–3)* → **[হাজিরা সেভ করুন]** *(1)* → snackbar "হাজিরা সেভ হয়েছে · পূর্বাবস্থায় ফেরান" and a return to Today, where the card now reads "✓ ২৮/৩০ উপস্থিত".

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ←  গণিত — দশম · ব্যাচ ক          ↶ undo  ⋮ │
│    ◀  বৃহস্পতিবার, ১৯ নভেম্বর · সকাল ৮:০০  ▶  │  date AND session time, always
│    [সকাল ৮:০০ ●] [বিকাল ৫:০০]   (only if 2+)  │  session chooser
│ ⓘ সবাই উপস্থিত ধরা হয়েছে। শুধু অনুপস্থিতদের    │
│   নামে চাপুন।                               │
│ [সবাই উপস্থিত] [সবাই অনুপস্থিত]   🔍 রোল/নাম │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ০১ (রা) রাকিব হাসান        ✓ উপস্থিত  [দেরি] │ green tint
│ ০২ (তা) তানিয়া আক্তার    ✕ অনুপস্থিত [দেরি] │ solid ink-slate
│ ০৩ (সা) সাদিয়া ইসলাম      ⏱ দেরি     [দেরি●]│ cyan tint
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  উপস্থিত ২৮ · অনুপস্থিত ১ · দেরি ১            │
│ [          হাজিরা সেভ করুন (৩০ জন)         ] │ sticky, 56dp
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Tapping anywhere on the row** toggles Present ↔ Absent. Rows are 64dp tall. A separate trailing **[দেরি]** button (48×48) toggles Late. **Long-press or ⋮** on a row opens: ছুটি (excused) and নোট. The teacher never has to cycle through 4 states.
- Each toggle gives a haptic tick and an instant colour + icon + word change, so a noisy room is no problem.
- **Save model:** the first save commits the day. After that, every change on that date **autosaves** and the header shows "সংরক্ষিত ✓". Before the first save, an unsaved draft survives app kills. Leaving the screen asks: "হাজিরা সেভ হয়নি। সেভ করবেন?" [সেভ করুন] [বাদ দিন].
- The **date arrows** are used to edit past days. A past-day banner reads "আপনি পুরোনো দিনের হাজিরা দেখছেন". Future dates are disabled.
- **Sessions:** a batch can have **2+ classes on one day** (exam season: morning + evening), and each has its own roll call.
  - The header always shows the date **and** the session time.
  - From Today or a notification, roll call opens on the exact session, so there is nothing to ask.
  - Anywhere roll call is opened from a batch (Batch › [হাজিরা নিন], the attendance history, the date arrows), a day with 2+ sessions first shows a simple chooser sheet "কোন ক্লাসের হাজিরা?" with big time cards [সকাল ৮:০০–৯:০০ · ✓ নেওয়া হয়েছে] [বিকাল ৫:০০–৬:০০ · বাকি]. The first untaken session is pre-highlighted. With 1 session the chooser never appears.
  - The session chips in the header switch between sessions without leaving the screen.
- **Desktop keys:** `↑/↓` or `J/K` move · `Space` toggle present/absent · `P` present · `A` absent · `L` late · `E` excused · typing digits jumps to that roll · `Ctrl+S` save · `Ctrl+Z` undo · `Alt+←/→` previous/next day. A shortcut legend sits in a collapsible right pane.

### (c) Record a payment — 3 taps
**From Dues:** row **[টাকা নিন]** *(1)* → bottom sheet (a right panel on desktop) *(prefilled)* → **[জমা করুন]** *(2)* → snackbar "৳৮০০ জমা হয়েছে" with actions **[রসিদ পাঠান]** / undo.
**From the student profile:** the batch section's **[টাকা নিন]** opens the same sheet.

```
┌ টাকা নিন ─────────────────────────────── ✕ ┐
│ (রা) রাকিব হাসান · গণিত দশম · ব্যাচ ক       │
│ কোন মাসের?  [অক্টোবর ✓] [নভেম্বর ✓] [ডিসে…]   │ multi-select chips
│             (বকেয়া মাসগুলো লাল দাগে চিহ্নিত)    │
│ পরিমাণ   ৳ [ ১,৬০০ ]   (২ মাস × ৳৮০০)        │
│ মাধ্যম   [ক্যাশ ●] [বিকাশ] [নগদ] [অন্যান্য]      │
│ তারিখ    আজ, ২০ নভেম্বর  ✎    নোট (ঐচ্ছিক) ✎    │
│ [      অক্টোবর–নভেম্বরের ৳১,৬০০ জমা করুন     ] │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Month default:** the oldest unpaid month is pre-checked. If nothing is owed, the next not-yet-paid month is pre-checked and the button reads "অগ্রিম". Chips show severity dots for unpaid months.
- **Amount default:** effective fee × selected months, using the fee override when one exists. Entering less shows inline "আংশিক — ৳৩০০ বাকি থাকবে". Entering more than the selected months asks "অতিরিক্ত ৳৮০০ পরের মাসের অগ্রিম হিসেবে রাখবেন?" [হ্যাঁ, অগ্রিম] [না, ঠিক করি].
- **Method:** remembers the last one used for that student. Cash is labelled **"ক্যাশ"**, never "নগদ" (see Trap #4).
- The button label repeats month + amount, so the teacher confirms by reading the button itself. No extra dialog.
- **Waive:** ⋮ in the sheet → "এই মাস মওকুফ করুন" → confirm dialog with an optional reason.

### (d) Chase unpaid fees (days 1–10)
Today → tap the **dues summary bar** *(1)* → Fees › বকেয়া, sorted by severity → on a row tap **📞** *(2)*, which opens the dialer with the guardian's number (falling back to the student's number), **or** tap **share** *(2)*, which opens the OS share sheet with the Bangla reminder pre-filled (WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS app — the teacher's choice).
- **Sort order:** overdue (most days first) → L5 → L1 → partial within level → amount descending. A sticky group header per level reads "মেয়াদোত্তীর্ণ (৩)", "শেষ সময় (২)"…
- **Filter chips:** সব · মেয়াদোত্তীর্ণ · এ মাসে · [ব্যাচ ▾].
- **Bulk:** "সবাইকে মনে করিয়ে দিন" walks through the list one share at a time (next ▶). No SMS gateway, as the contract requires.
- After sharing, the row shows "আজ মনে করানো হয়েছে" (the day's reminder log), so the teacher doesn't nag twice.

Dues row (phone):
```
│▌(রা) রাকিব হাসান            ৳১,৬০০  [⚠ জরুরি · ৭ দিন] │  ▌= 4dp level bar
│▌ গণিত দশম · ব্যাচ ক · অক্টোবর, নভেম্বর                 │
│▌ [📞 কল]   [↗ মনে করান]            [ টাকা নিন ]        │
```

### (e) Add a student to a batch, and to a second batch
- **New student:** Batch detail › শিক্ষার্থী › FAB **[+ শিক্ষার্থী]** → the sheet opens with a **search-first field**: "নাম বা ফোন লিখুন". As the teacher types, existing matches appear on top: "রাকিব হাসান · ০১৭১…৫৬৭ · পদার্থ ব্যাচ খ — **এই ব্যাচে যোগ করুন**". If there is no match → **"নতুন শিক্ষার্থী: '{typed}'"** → short form (name, guardian phone; "আরও তথ্য" expands roll, student phone, guardian name, admission date [today], fee override, photo, notes) → **[সেভ করুন]**. About 4 taps plus typing.
- **Second batch from the profile:** Student profile → **[+ অন্য ব্যাচে ভর্তি]** → batch picker grouped by subject, with the student's current batches disabled and marked ✓ → optional fee override → **[যোগ করুন]**. *(3 taps)*
- **Duplicate guard:** saving a "new" student whose name *and* phone match an existing one, or whose phone alone matches, shows: "এই নম্বরে আগে থেকে 'রাকিব হাসান' আছে। তাকেই এই ব্যাচে যোগ করবেন?" [হ্যাঁ, তাকেই যোগ করুন] [না, নতুন শিক্ষার্থী].

### (f) Backup export / import
**More › ব্যাকআপ**. The screen shows "শেষ ব্যাকআপ: ১২ দিন আগে". When this is older than 7 days it turns into an amber info chip. This chip is not a dues colour; it uses a neutral `info` style.
- **Export:** **[ব্যাকআপ ফাইল তৈরি করুন]** → progress → "ফাইল তৈরি হয়েছে: tuition-backup-2026-11-20.db" → [শেয়ার/সেভ করুন] (OS share sheet or save dialog on Windows). *(2 taps)*
- **Import:** **[ব্যাকআপ থেকে ফিরিয়ে আনুন]** → file picker → **confirmation screen** (full screen, never a small dialog):
```
┌ ব্যাকআপ যাচাই ────────────────────────────┐
│ ফাইল: tuition-backup-2026-11-18.db           │
│ তৈরি: ১৮ নভেম্বর ২০২৬, রাত ৯:১৫ · Windows PC   │
│ এই ব্যাকআপে ৪৭টি পরিবর্তন আছে                  │  ← headline, 24sp
│                নতুন   বদলানো   মুছে যাবে        │
│ ব্যাচ            ০       ১        ০            │
│ শিক্ষার্থী          ৩       ২        ১            │
│ হাজিরা           ৩৮      ০        ০            │
│ পেমেন্ট           ২       ০        ০            │
│ ⚠ ১ জন শিক্ষার্থী মুছে যাবে: তানিয়া আক্তার  ▸   │ deletions always listed by name
│ ⓘ প্রয়োগের আগে আপনার বর্তমান তথ্যের একটি কপি     │
│   স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে রাখা হবে।                       │
│ [বাতিল]                [৪৭টি পরিবর্তন প্রয়োগ করুন]│
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- If the backup is **older** than the current data: amber banner "এই ব্যাকআপ আপনার বর্তমান তথ্যের চেয়ে পুরোনো".
- If there are **0 changes**: "এই ব্যাকআপ ও আপনার তথ্য একই — কিছু করার নেই" [ঠিক আছে].
- If the file is invalid: "এই ফাইলটি এই অ্যাপের ব্যাকআপ নয়" [অন্য ফাইল বেছে নিন].
- After applying: "৪৭টি পরিবর্তন প্রয়োগ হয়েছে" plus **[আগের অবস্থায় ফেরান]**, which restores the automatic pre-import copy.

│ আজকের রুটিন                  [+ অতিরিক্ত ক্লাস] │

- The day strip shows Saturday to Friday. Swiping changes the week, and a **[আজ]** chip jumps back to today.
- Picking another day turns the hero card into "বৃহস্পতিবার, ২৬ নভেম্বর — ৩টি ক্লাস", and the dues bar is hidden.
- Class-state pills use **neutral `statePill`** tones with icons, so they never borrow dues or attendance colours:
  - ● এখন চলছে (Now)
  - ↻ সময় বদলেছে (Moved)
  - ⊘ বাতিল (Cancelled, struck through and dimmed)
  - ＋ অতিরিক্ত (Extra)

**Desktop week grid (≥ 840dp, Today › সপ্তাহ):**
- Layout: 7 columns (শনি–শুক্র) × hour rows, auto-ranging from 1 h before the earliest class to 1 h after the latest.
- 15-min gridlines are drawn faint, hour lines in `hairline`, and the "now" line in `accent`.
- Class blocks are flat `accentContainer` on a `surfaceDense` grid, with the subject — batch name and time. Two sessions of one batch on a day are two separate blocks, each labelled with its time. Cancelled blocks get a dashed outline and struck-through text. Extra classes show a "＋" label, and moved classes show a ghost outline at the old time.
- Clicking a block opens a popover with the class actions. Clicking an empty cell offers "+ এই সময়ে অতিরিক্ত ক্লাস".
- Keyboard: arrow keys move between blocks, `Enter` opens the actions, `N` starts a new extra class, `[`/`]` change the week.

**Edit weekly slots** (Batch › সময়সূচি › **[প্রতি সপ্তাহের সময় বদলান]**): this opens the same slot editor as onboarding.
- With "সব দিনে একই সময়" OFF, each day is a group with one row per time slot:
```
শনি   সকাল ৮:০০ – ৯:০০     ✎ 🗑
      বিকাল ৫:০০ – ৬:০০    ✎ 🗑
      + আরেকটি সময়
সোম   সকাল ৮:০০ – ৯:০০     ✎ 🗑
      + আরেকটি সময়
+ আরেকটি দিন
```
- Slots of the **same batch** on the same day are also checked for overlap: "এই দিনে এই ব্যাচের আরেকটি ক্লাস সকাল ৮:০০–৯:০০" + fix. Touching slots are fine.
- The live sentence groups slots by day: "শনি — সকাল ৮:০০ ও বিকাল ৫:০০; সোম — সকাল ৮:০০".
- Saving asks "কবে থেকে নতুন সময়?" [আজ থেকে ●] [তারিখ বাছুন]. One-off changes already made to future dates are kept.

**Bangla time picker** (bottom sheet; a dropdown panel on desktop):
- Row 1: period segmented control **[সকাল][দুপুর][বিকাল][সন্ধ্যা][রাত]**.
  - Fixed ranges: সকাল 5:00–11:59, দুপুর 12:00–2:59, বিকাল 3:00–5:59, সন্ধ্যা 6:00–7:59, রাত 8:00–4:59.
  - The period chip opens pre-set to the current value.
- Row 2: a chip grid of only that period's hours (e.g. সকাল → ৫ ৬ ৭ ৮ ৯ ১০ ১১).
- Row 3: minute chips **[:০০][:১৫][:৩০][:৪৫]**, plus "অন্য মিনিট" for any minute.
- The big readout always shows the period ("সন্ধ্যা ৭:৩০"), so 7 am and 7 pm can never be confused.
- English mode uses the same sheet with **[AM][PM]** in place of the five periods and shows "7:30 PM".
- Desktop also accepts typing: "7:30 pm", "19:30" or "৭:৩০ সন্ধ্যা" are parsed, and the readout echoes the parsed time.

**Class actions** (≤ 3 taps; class card ⋮ or long-press → sheet titled with the date: "শুধু শুক্রবার, ২০ নভেম্বর"):
- **Reschedule:** ⋮ *(1)* → **সময় বদলান** *(2)* → sheet with the date and time prefilled (same duration). Change the time → **[সেভ করুন]** *(3)*. The card then shows "↻ সময় বদলেছে". The only scope is "this date only", because permanent changes live in the batch.
- **Cancel:** ⋮ *(1)* → **এই দিনের ক্লাস বাতিল** *(2)*. There is no dialog; a snackbar shows "ক্লাস বাতিল হয়েছে · পূর্বাবস্থায় ফেরান" plus **[অভিভাবকদের জানান]**, which opens the share sheet with the cancel message. The reminder for that class is removed.
- **Extra class:** **[+ অতিরিক্ত ক্লাস]** *(1)* → sheet: batch (pre-filled when started from a batch or a card), date (the selected day), time, duration → **[যোগ করুন]** *(2–3)*.
  - It is **allowed even when that batch already has a class that day**. The sheet just notes "এই দিনে এই ব্যাচের আরও ১টি ক্লাস আছে: সকাল ৮:০০" for awareness.
  - Only the normal no-overlap conflict check applies.
- **Restore:** a cancelled or moved card's ⋮ shows **"আগের সময়ে ফেরত নিন"**.
- **Roll call** for a cancelled class is hidden. For a moved class, roll call uses the new time.
- **Multiple sessions a day:** each session is its own card, its own roll call, its own reminder, and has its own cancel/move actions.
  - Cancelling the morning session never touches the evening one, and the sheet title says so: "শুধু ২০ নভেম্বর · সকাল ৮:০০".
  - On those cards the time is shown **inline with the batch name** at titleMedium weight ("গণিত দশম · ব্যাচ ক · সকাল ৮:০০" vs "… · বিকাল ৫:০০"), as well as in the time column, so the two are never confused.

**Conflict UI.** The rule is a hard block: two classes conflict when their [start, end) times overlap on the same date, across all active batches. Touching times are fine.
- **Live check:** the conflict is checked while editing, the moment a time is chosen, not only on save.
- **Inline card:** an `errorContainer` card appears directly under the time field. It is friendly, names the clash, and offers a fix:
```
╭ ⚠ এই সময়ে আপনার আরেকটি ক্লাস আছে ──────────────────╮
│ রসায়ন — দ্বাদশ · ব্যাচ খ                                │
│ রবিবার, ২২ নভেম্বর · সকাল ১০:০০–১১:০০                      │
│ [ সকাল ১১:০০–১২:০০ নিন ]   [ আগে: সকাল ৮:৩০–৯:৩০ ]          │
╰──────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
```
- **Suggested fixes:** the nearest free slot with the same duration on the same date. The later option is the primary button and the earlier option is secondary. Tapping one fills the time and the card disappears. "Touching" suggestions are allowed: a class ending at 11:00 suggests 11:00.
- **Weekly slot edits** check every weekday the slot repeats on, plus future one-off changes. The card names the first clash and adds "+২টি আরও ▸" (expandable).
- **Save button:** it stays visible but disabled, with the reason under it: "আগে সময়ের মিল ঠিক করুন". Saving cannot be forced.
- **Wording:** never a cryptic error, and never the words "conflict" or "overlap" in Bangla mode.

### (h) Class reminders
- **Global settings** (Settings › ক্লাস ও রিমাইন্ডার):
  - Master switch **"ক্লাসের আগে মনে করিয়ে দিন"**, default ON once permission is granted.
  - **"ডিফল্ট সময়: ১৫ মিনিট আগে ▸"**.
- **Per-batch control** (Batch › সময়সূচি):
  - The row reads **"রিমাইন্ডার: ডিফল্ট (১৫ মিনিট আগে) ▸"**, and the default value is always shown in brackets.
  - Tapping it opens a sheet with radio options:
    - ● **ডিফল্ট ব্যবহার করুন (১৫ মিনিট)**, which stores null
    - ৫ · ১০ · ১৫ · ২০ · ৩০ · ৬০ মিনিট আগে
    - **অন্য…** (a number field, 1–180 min, accepting ০-৯ and 0-9)
  - Once set, the row reads "রিমাইন্ডার: ২০ মিনিট আগে". A ↺ "ডিফল্টে ফেরান" link restores null.
  - When the master switch is OFF, the row is disabled and reads "রিমাইন্ডার বন্ধ আছে — সেটিংস থেকে চালু করুন ▸".
- **Permission priming** (full screen on the mesh + grain backdrop, with a glass card; shown at onboarding step 4, or the first time reminders are switched on):
```
            (bell + clock illustration, flat line art)
  ক্লাসের আগে মনে করিয়ে দেব
  • প্রতিটি ক্লাসের ১৫ মিনিট আগে ফোনে একটি নোটিফিকেশন আসবে
  • নোটিফিকেশনে চাপ দিলেই ওই ব্যাচের হাজিরা খুলবে
  • সময় পরে বদলানো যাবে
  [ হ্যাঁ, চালু করুন ]          এখন না
```
- **What happens after "হ্যাঁ, চালু করুন":**
  - The OS notification prompt appears (Android 13+).
  - If exact alarms aren't allowed, a second short card follows: "সময়মতো রিমাইন্ডারের জন্য আরেকটি অনুমতি লাগবে: 'অ্যালার্ম ও রিমাইন্ডার' চালু করুন" **[সেটিংস খুলুন]**. The app detects the return and shows "✓ সব ঠিক আছে".
  - If a permission is denied, the Settings row shows an `info` chip "অনুমতি নেই — রিমাইন্ডার আসবে না [ঠিক করুন]". The app never nags with repeated dialogs.
- **Other platforms:** Windows needs no priming (toast notifications). On Web the section shows "রিমাইন্ডার শুধু অ্যান্ড্রয়েড ও উইন্ডোজ অ্যাপে কাজ করে".
- **[একটি টেস্ট রিমাইন্ডার পাঠান]** in Settings sends a sample notification immediately.
- **Notification copy and behaviour:** the copy is in §7. Tapping the notification, or its **[হাজিরা নিন]** action, opens that batch's roll call for that date. Cancelled classes never notify. Moved and extra classes notify at their actual time.

### (i) Theme
- Settings › চেহারা › থিম offers [সিস্টেম ●] [লাইট] [ডার্ক]. The change applies instantly with a crossfade, and there is no restart or confirm.

### 3a. Settings screen (More tab)
**Rule: include only what a teacher would genuinely change.** The final list belongs to Product; this is the layout contract.
- One scrolling screen with no nested settings pages deeper than one sheet. Aim for ≤ 18 rows in total.

### Legibility law (glass vs dense)
- **Glass** (`glass`/`glassNav`) is allowed **only** on cards, navigation and sheet chrome that sit directly over the mesh.
- Anything **dense** sits on `surfaceDense`, even inside a glass card: lists, roll-call rows, dues rows, forms, tables, dialogs and sheet bodies. A glass card holding a list switches its fill to `surfaceDense`, so the card alpha rises for dense content.
- Severity and attendance badges are **flat and opaque** (§5–6). Their contrast is text-on-own-fill and never depends on what is behind them.
- Text never sits directly on the bare mesh except the big screen title, which uses textPrimary and is ≥ 12:1 on every pool.

### Flutter feasibility & performance rules
1. **Blur budget:** at most **3 `BackdropFilter` regions per screen** (typically nav + 1–2 top cards or a sheet). Wrap each in `ClipRRect`, and use `BackdropGroup` where available.
2. **No blur inside scrolling lists.** Cards in scrollables use `glassNoBlur` (translucent fill only).
3. **Mesh:** 3 stacked `RadialGradient` layers in a `DecoratedBox` at the app root, inside a `RepaintBoundary`. It is painted once per window-size or theme change and is **static**, never animated.
4. **Grain:** a 128×128 noise tile generated **once at startup** from a fixed seed (3-octave value noise) and cached as a `ui.Image`. It is drawn with `ImageShader(TileMode.repeated)`, `BlendMode.overlay`, at 5% / 7% opacity, in the same `RepaintBoundary`. No asset is needed.
5. **Reduced effects path:** no blur (glass → `surfaceSolid` @ 92%) and no grain; the mesh is kept because it is cheap and static.
   - It turns on automatically when `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` or `highContrast` is set, or on an Android low-RAM device (platform-channel check).
   - On web it turns on if, after the first 120 frames, average raster time exceeds 12 ms. The check runs once and the result is remembered.
   - There is also a manual toggle in Settings › চেহারা › "কম ইফেক্ট". Layout is identical with or without it.
6. **Budget:** raster ≤ 8 ms/frame on a mid-range Android device while scrolling the dues list. CI golden tests cover both themes × both effect modes.
7. **Desktop scaling:** the mesh always fills the **entire window** behind all panes. Pools stay anchored to their corners and edge. On wide windows, pool radii are % × the **shorter side** S (A: 1.2S × 0.72S, B: 1.04S × 0.64S, C: 1.28S × 0.72S), so pools never stretch into bands.

## 5. Payment warning severity system (the heart)

**Due date:** prepaid month M → 1st of M. Postpaid month M → 1st of M+1. **Day N** = days since the due date + 1, so the due date itself is day 1. For a student with several unpaid months, the badge uses the **oldest unpaid month**, and the row adds "৩ মাস বাকি".

**Escalation hue** (Aurora Ink): honey → amber → tangerine → coral → rose, then solid raspberry for overdue. The hue colours the 10-segment bar and the row edge bars. Badges use readable tints of the same hue.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | 1–2 | নতুন বকেয়া | Just due | `notifications` | `#D9A520` honey | `#FBEFC9` / `#5E4300` (≈8.0:1) | `#3A2E0A` / `#F5CF6A` (≈8.9:1) |

**Shape rule:** L1–L4 are tinted badges. L5 is tinted with a strong rose border. **Overdue is the only solid badge**, identical in both themes, so "the solid raspberry one" is always the worst. Text contrast is AA or better everywhere. The hue bars are decorative and supplemental, because the badge label carries the meaning.

**10-segment escalation bar** (dues card on Today and the Fees header):
- Segment *d* (d = 1…10) is coloured by its level hue. Days already passed are filled at 100%; future days use `track`.
- **Today's segment is ringed**: a 2px textPrimary ring with a 2px gap. "আজ ৫ তারিখ" is written below it, and the scale ends are labelled "১" and "১০".
- When the oldest unpaid fee is beyond day 10, all 10 segments fill and a solid **মেয়াদোত্তীর্ণ** badge sits at the right end.
- Semantics: "বকেয়ার ৫ম দিন, ১০ দিনের মধ্যে".

## 8. Accessibility rules
- **Targets:** min 48×48dp for every interactive element, with 8dp spacing between adjacent targets. Primary buttons are 56dp tall. Attendance rows are 64dp.
- **Contrast:** body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text/icons/UI boundaries ≥ 3:1. All badge pairs in §5–6 meet AA. CI includes a token contrast test.
- **Never colour alone:** every status = icon + word (+ day count). This was tested in grayscale and protanopia/deuteranopia simulation: L1–L4 also differ by icon shape, and L5/OD by solid fill.
- **Semantics:** each row has one merged label, e.g. "রাকিব হাসান, রোল ১, উপস্থিত। অনুপস্থিত করতে দুবার ট্যাপ করুন" / "Rakib Hasan, roll 1, present. Double-tap to mark absent." A badge reads "জরুরি, ৭ দিন বকেয়া, ৳১,৬০০". Live counters use live regions. Decorative edge bars are excluded from semantics.
- **Text scale:** layouts hold up at 1.3× without clipping. Rows grow in height and never clip Bangla matras (no fixed-height text boxes).
- **Focus order:** top bar → filters → list (in visual order) → sticky primary action. In sheets: first field → … → primary button. Opening a sheet moves focus into it, and closing it returns focus to the trigger.
- **Desktop keyboard:** visible 2dp `accent` focus ring, full Tab/Shift+Tab traversal, Enter activates, Esc closes sheet/dialog. Global shortcuts: `Ctrl+1…5` destinations, `Ctrl+F` search (`Ctrl+K` alias), `Ctrl+N` new (contextual: batch/student), `Ctrl+P` collect payment on the selected student, plus the roll-call keys in §2b. A shortcuts sheet opens with `?`.
- **Motion:** no flashing or pulsing for overdue, and no animated gradients. Respect "reduce motion" (crossfades only; the theme switch becomes instant).
- **Both themes, both effect modes:** every screen is QA'd in Light and Dark, with full effects and reduced effects. Golden tests cover theme × language × effects. Text on glass is checked at the worst point (glass over the brightest pool), and hero text is checked at the teal pool centre.
- **Reduced transparency:** when the OS asks for it (or "কম ইফেক্ট" is on), glass becomes `surfaceSolid` and the grain is removed (§4).
- **Time & schedule:** a time is always announced with its period ("সন্ধ্যা সাতটা ত্রিশ" / "7:30 PM"). Week-grid blocks have full labels: "গণিত দশম ব্যাচ ক, রবিবার সকাল ১০টা থেকে ১১টা, বাতিল". A conflict card is a live region announced when it appears, and focus moves to its first fix button.
- **Language:** a locale is set per text span so TalkBack/Narrator uses Bangla voice for Bangla text.

## 9. Where the naive tester will get stuck — and the fix

1. **"Do I have to tap every student who is here?"** Default-all-present is invisible if it isn't stated. **Fix:** a permanent hint banner on the roll call screen plus a live counter ("উপস্থিত ৩০") that visibly decreases as he taps. The first-run coach-mark says it once more.
2. **"I took attendance but it's gone."** The classic forgot-to-save problem. **Fix:** a sticky save button that shows the count, draft persistence across app kills, a leave-guard dialog, autosave after the first save, and a Today card that flips to "✓ ২৮/৩০" as proof.
3. **"Prepaid? Postpaid? What?"** Billing jargon. **Fix:** never show those words. Use two radio cards in plain Bangla with a concrete month example, default to advance (the most common practice), and allow changes later with a "কোন মাস থেকে?" prompt. The batch header pill repeats the plain phrase.
4. **"I chose নগদ for cash and it saved as Nagad"** — and its cousin, "I paid the wrong month". In Bangla, "নগদ" means both cash and the Nagad wallet. **Fix:** cash is always labelled **"ক্যাশ"** with a banknote icon, and Nagad has a phone-wallet icon. For months, the oldest unpaid month is pre-selected, unpaid month chips carry severity dots, and the confirm button spells out the month and amount ("অক্টোবরের ৳৮০০ জমা করুন"). Undo is available for 8 s, and payments can be edited or deleted from the log.
5. **"I added Rakib again for his physics batch and now there are two Rakibs."** **Fix:** the add-student field is search-first and offers existing students at the top ("এই ব্যাচে যোগ করুন"). The profile has a clear "অন্য ব্যাচে ভর্তি" button. Saving triggers a phone/name duplicate check that offers to reuse the existing student.

6. **"I set the class for 7 and it's at 7 in the morning!"** AM/PM slips are classic. **Fix:** the time picker requires a period (সকাল/দুপুর/বিকাল/সন্ধ্যা/রাত) before an hour can be chosen, and the hour grid only shows that period's hours. Every time on screen carries its period, and a live sentence confirms the choice: "প্রতি শনি, সোম, বুধ — সন্ধ্যা ৭:০০ থেকে ৮:০০".
7. **"It won't let me save — is the app broken?"** A hard-blocked conflict feels like a bug. **Fix:** the clash is caught live, before tapping save. A friendly card names the other class and its time and offers the nearest free slot as a one-tap button. The disabled save button says why under itself.
8. **"I changed today's time and now it's changed every week"** (or the reverse). **Fix:** two clearly separated places. Class-card actions are titled "শুধু {তারিখ}" and only ever affect that date. Weekly changes live in Batch › সময়সূচি behind "প্রতি সপ্তাহের সময় বদলান" and ask "কবে থেকে?". Moved and cancelled cards show the original time and offer a one-tap restore.
9. **"The reminder never came."** Denied permission, exact-alarm restrictions, or the master switch being off. **Fix:** the priming screen explains the benefit before the OS prompt. Settings shows a missing-permission chip with [ঠিক করুন] and offers **[টেস্ট রিমাইন্ডার পাঠান]**. The per-batch row always states the effective value, or that reminders are off and where to turn them on.
10. **"Dark mode looks broken / I can't read the badge."** **Fix:** the legibility law. Glass appears only on cards and navigation; every list, form, roll-call row and table sits on near-opaque `surfaceDense`. Badges are flat and opaque with AA contrast in both themes. "কম ইফেক্ট" kicks in automatically on weak phones. A visible theme control (সিস্টেম / লাইট / ডার্ক) has a preview, and System is the default.

*Bonus guard (backup):* fear of wiping data on import. The full-screen change summary lists deletions by name, an automatic pre-import copy is kept, and one-tap "আগের অবস্থায় ফেরান" restores it.
